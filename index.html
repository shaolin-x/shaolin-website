Biography

I am a PhD student in computer science at the University of Southern California, advised by Prof. Ibrahim Sabek in the NEXDIG Lab. My research focuses on building next‑generation data systems and multi‑agent systems. I am especially interested in lifelong query optimization, geospatial reasoning, and collaborative data management.

I am always open to collaborations and discussions. Feel free to reach out if you are interested in data systems, multi‑agent systems, or related topics.

Interests
